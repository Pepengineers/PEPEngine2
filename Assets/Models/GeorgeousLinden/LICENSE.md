# Linden tree by Georgeous

The two FBX meshes and four PNG textures are from [Linden tree by Georgeous (intice184)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/linden-tree-c0c3aa73cc9945d2a36c01a0d8d6d4bb), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The source files are unmodified. Bistro places and scales the tree at import and animates the foliage at runtime. `SM_Linden.fbx` is the author's LOD0 and `SM_Linden_.fbx` is LOD1.
